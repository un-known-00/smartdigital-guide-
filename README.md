# SmartDigital Guide — AdSense-ready starter website

## Included
- Responsive homepage
- Article page with original starter article
- About page
- Contact page
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Responsive CSS and mobile navigation
- Clearly marked ad placeholder on the article page

## Before publishing
1. Replace `onlineworker426@gmail.com` with your real email.
2. Replace the placeholder contact form with a real form handler.
3. Add your real domain, favicon, logo and social profiles.
4. Add several more genuinely useful, original articles.
5. Add a cookie/consent solution appropriate to the countries you serve, especially before enabling advertising.
6. When approved/ready, replace the ad placeholder with the AdSense code supplied by Google. Do not click your own ads or encourage others to click them.
7. Review current Google AdSense policies and your local legal requirements before applying.

This starter is designed to provide the structure; AdSense approval is not guaranteed.
